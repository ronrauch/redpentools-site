Red Pen Tools Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

This policy covers Red Pen Tools, VFX Shot List Maker, VFX Shot Puller and VFX Title Maker on Mac, iPhone and iPad, made by CustomTools.App LLC ("we").

The short version

We don't collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking. The free version of Red Pen Tools on iPhone and iPad shows ads from Google; see Ads below. The classic apps and Red Pen Tools on the Mac show no ads.

Your files

The EDLs, AAFs, bins and other files you open are read on your device. What you export is saved where you choose: on your device or in your own iCloud Drive. We never receive your files.

iCloud Drive

If you use the app's folders in iCloud Drive, your files are stored in your own iCloud account, run by Apple. We cannot see them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Ads

Red Pen Tools on iPhone and iPad is free with a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. The ads are non-personalized: we never ask to track you, and the app has no accounts, so nothing Google receives is tied to your name or email. To show and count ads, Google's software collects a device identifier, which ads were shown and tapped, your approximate location from your IP address, and diagnostic data. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. In the EU, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time under Ad privacy choices.

Unlock All, a one-time purchase, removes the ads. After you buy it, the app no longer contacts Google's ad servers on any of your devices.

Crash reports

If you share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
