Getting started with Red Pen Tools 2.0

The same app runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

  1. Open a file from Files or the Finder, or drop it on the app.
  2. Pick a tool.
  3. Exports land in the Out folder. AAFs from Auto Cut-In and saved ALEs land in To Avid.

Where your folders are

  • iPhone and iPad: iCloud Drive > Red Pen Tools. When iCloud Drive is off, look in On My iPhone or On My iPad > Red Pen Tools.
  • Mac: the folders live inside the app. Use Share to copy a file somewhere else, or link a folder of your own.

You can link a Dropbox, LucidLink or shared folder.

AAF and Avid bin files are read on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

The 19 tools

Four groups, in the order the app lists them.

EDL

  • Shot Lister: EDL to the VFX shot list, TSV or CSV
  • EDL Inspector: title, frame rate, events, dissolves, speed changes, markers, CDL
  • ALE Inspector: columns and rows of an ALE; merge, CSV, shot list
  • Change List: what changed between two EDLs
  • Pull List Generator: source pulls with handles
  • Vendor Assignment: shots by scene to vendors, per-vendor lists
  • Sequence Markers: VFX IDs and markers for every shot
  • Version Updater: versions from clip names into a tracker

SubCaps and Titles

  • EDL to SubCap: EDL markers and clips to Avid SubCap
  • SubCap to Markers: SubCap to an Avid marker list
  • VFX Title Maker: VFX markers to SubCap titles, XML and CSV
  • Status SubCap: pipeline status on SubCap IDs

AAF and Avid

  • VFX Lineup Maker: AAF clips to a lineup with effects
  • AAF Inspector: tracks, markers and clips inside an AAF
  • EDL + AAF Check: an EDL against the AAF of the same sequence
  • Auto Cut-In: comps placed on the timeline as an AAF

Trackers and Utilities

  • Shot Merger: timecodes from an update into the master
  • VFX Shot Puller: ffmpeg commands to cut shots by VFX ID, run on a computer; plus Pull Slips PDF and CSV
  • Speed Calculator: speed effects, TC to frames, TC math

The classic apps

VFX Shot List Maker, VFX Shot Puller and VFX Title Maker each carry one of the three original tools: Shot List, Shot Puller and Title Maker. They work the same way as the matching tools in Red Pen Tools.

The same support email covers them: ron@customtools.app

Quick start: Shot Lister

  1. Download RPT_101_SAMPLE.edl.
  2. Open Red Pen Tools and pick Shot Lister.
  3. Choose File, then pick RPT_101_SAMPLE.edl.
  4. Generate Shot List.
  5. Save CSV.
  6. Find the CSV in the Out folder.
